# Support Directory Verification — Addiction and Service Details (2026-10)

## Changes in this batch

- Replaced the legacy `1800-11-0031` drug de-addiction helpline entry with **14446**, which is listed by India's Department of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- Removed unverified fixed OPD hours and emergency-casualty wording from the NIMHANS entry. Readers are directed to confirm current hours and services with NIMHANS.
- Replaced guessed district mental-health programme timings and broad service guarantees with instructions to confirm local availability with the relevant government hospital.
- Clarified that de-addiction centre services, admission options, fees, eligibility, and language availability vary by centre and should be checked before travelling.
- Updated Vandrevala Foundation language/cost descriptions to reflect its official service information without claiming that every language is available through every channel.

6. NIMHANS official contact page: https://www.nimhans.ac.in/contact-us

## Safety and limitations

- A national helpline listing does not establish that every local rehabilitation centre is currently open or eligible for every patient.
- No district-level phone numbers, local schedules, or fees were invented.
- Readers should contact a centre before travelling and use emergency services for immediate danger.
- This is a source-backed directory correction, not a guarantee of response time or service outcome.
