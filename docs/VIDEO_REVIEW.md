## Safety and privacy checks

- Video IDs must match the 11-character YouTube ID format before any embed is rendered.
- The player uses a click-to-play facade and the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain; no iframe is created until the user presses play.
- Video title and publisher attribution are shown with the player.
- The current curated set is English-language. Hindi/Gujarati captions, regional playback, and ongoing availability are not certified by automated tests.
- CI checks ID format and source metadata only; it does not verify that YouTube videos remain online or that their content/captions are unchanged.
- Recheck publisher, title, video availability, caption accuracy, and clinical suitability before each release. Do not invent IDs or imply a video is endorsed by a professional body unless the publisher and title are verified.
- No independent clinical sign-off is claimed in this register.
