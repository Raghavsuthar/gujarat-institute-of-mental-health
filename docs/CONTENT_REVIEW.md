# Clinical content review register

**Last source-check date:** 2026-10-24  
**Scope:** Evidence-guide summaries added in Phase 5, batch 2.  
**Review status:** Source links were checked against public guidance during authoring. This register does **not** claim independent consultant/clinician sign-off; a named clinical reviewer should be recorded only after that review actually occurs.

## Editorial rules

- Keep content educational and non-diagnostic; do not give individualized medication instructions.
- Distinguish a screening prompt from a diagnosis and mention important limitations.
- Use respectful, non-stigmatizing wording; physical symptoms are not described as fake or imaginary.
- Keep English, Hindi, and Gujarati copy aligned in meaning.
- Use the source organization's own guidance scope. Do not generalize a disorder-specific guideline to unrelated diagnoses.
- Recheck source status before future revisions; links and recommendations can change.
