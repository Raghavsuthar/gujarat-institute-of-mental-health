## Safety and editorial boundaries

- If someone is in immediate danger, at risk of acting on suicidal thoughts, has taken an overdose, or needs urgent medical help, advise contacting emergency services / attending the nearest emergency department and involving a trusted person where safe.
- Tele-MANAS is a mental-health support and counselling pathway; do not describe it as a substitute for emergency rescue.
- The code change does not claim every detail of each service has been independently tested across all telecom operators or locations.
- Other resource entries still contain unresolved `[VERIFY]` placeholders and must be reviewed individually before being presented as verified.
- This change does not establish clinical sign-off. Keep the source and date visible in the editorial register.
