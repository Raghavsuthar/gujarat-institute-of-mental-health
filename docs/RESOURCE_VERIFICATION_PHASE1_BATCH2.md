## Safety and editorial boundaries

- These are directory corrections, not an endorsement of any service and not a guarantee that a call will connect in every situation.
- If someone is in immediate danger, has attempted suicide, has taken an overdose, or needs urgent medical care, use emergency services or the nearest emergency department.
- Tele-MANAS is a mental-health support and counselling pathway, not a replacement for emergency response.
- Remaining resource records still contain unresolved `[VERIFY]` markers. They must be individually checked before their numbers, hours, costs, languages, eligibility, and service details are presented as confirmed.
- No claim of clinical sign-off is made.
